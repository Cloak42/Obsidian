## Intro:
phase 1:
Using a [[Door sensor]] triggers this webcam to take a photo of the door (and whoever entered the room), using an onboard object detection program it will zero in on the face of the person and stream that image to the [[Home Lab]] where an ai model will be trained to recognize different faces.  when fully implemented this system will be able to tell me who entered my room at what time, as well as be able to recognize me and whoever else i designate as non intruders and not notify me when they enter

phase 2:
After the initial phase has been completed i want to use this to set up some automation.  cross referencing who the person that entered the room with a schedule i could get the room to turn on the lights for me as i enter the room at any time passed sunset.  and turn them off as i leave the room.  for fun i could also include an intruder routine which could include flashing lights and turning on a speaker playing specific audio.
